## 1. Purpose

This Crystal defines how reusable skills are structured and maintained in Dragon 2.0.

## 2. Core Definition

A **Skill** is a focused, reusable procedure or capability that an AI instance can load when needed.

Skills are not the operating definition (that lives in the Shard).  
They are modular tools that support specific kinds of work.
