## 1. Purpose

This Crystal defines Ocular and Spec-Lens modules — the interchangeable ends of the Looking Glass.

## 2. Core Definitions

**Ocular**  
How the human sends and receives information (voice, text, device, interruption patterns, accessibility needs).

**Spec-Lens**  
What a particular AI / interface / environment can actually see and do, including observed limits and accumulated findings.

A new or special Spec-Lens may be created when needed.

Looking Glass (the interaction contract) lives as a section inside the Shard.
