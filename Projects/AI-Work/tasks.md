# tasks

**Purpose:** daily or recurring runs, and work that depends on a bot or on the owner. Each task has a name, a date, who it belongs to, what triggers it, and the steps. Repeatable procedures live in `processes.md`.

**Promote to a process:** a task with implications for later work, in another job or another process, is elevated to a process in `processes.md`. A task you repeat can also be upgraded to a process.

The two tasks below are examples. Replace them with your own.

## Task 1: Daily in-progress list mirror (example)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Belongs to:** a bot (the one that keeps your files organized)
- **Trigger:** every day at a time you choose.
- **Steps:** 1. Use the local in-progress list (rebuild it from your history folders if it is missing or empty). 2. Archive the previous stored copy to the history folder first (Process 3). 3. Write the new copy. 4. Read it back to verify. 5. Message the owner only on failure or a rebuilt list. Nothing is deleted.

## Task 2: Shared reminders (example)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Belongs to:** the owner and the assistant
- **Trigger:** whenever the owner or a bot adds a reminder; reviewed at the start of a session.
- **Steps:** 1. Reminders are kept in one file in your Areas folder. 2. A bot proposes a new reminder as a draft. 3. It is written only after the owner's permission, archive-first. 4. Done items are marked, not deleted.
